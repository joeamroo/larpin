## Listing

Name: LarpIn
Tagline (48 chars): LinkedIn, but everyone admits they're larping
Link: https://larpin.io
Topics: Social Media, Humor, Open Source, Side Project

Description:
A fully working parody social network. You get a fake persona on arrival (no
signup), post larps to one global feed, connect and DM other larpers, and
climb the Larpboard, a leaderboard of openly inflated follower counts. Posts
get a Larp Level scored on buzzword density. Reactions include Grindset,
Labubu, and Larp Larp Larp Sahur. Premium costs $0, the gold badge does
nothing, and Search is gated behind it anyway. An AI button rewrites your
draft into maximum hustle-cringe. Open source (MIT), built on Rails 8.

performance art, and somebody had to make it official.

It's a real social network where everything is a bit. Registration happens TO
you: land on the site and you're instantly "Chadwick Sterling III, Serial
Founder | Exited (emotionally) | Raising a pre-idea round." Post your larps,
connect with other larpers, DM anyone (bots reply instantly), react with
