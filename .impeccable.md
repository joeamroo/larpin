# Impeccable Design Context - LarpIn

## Design Context

### Users
Hacker News visitors and terminally-online professionals arriving from a "Show HN" link. They land cold, decide in 10 seconds whether the joke is good, and either post a larp or bounce. Mostly desktop, meaningful mobile share. The job to be done: get the joke instantly, write one funny fake post, share it.

### Brand Personality
Corporate-earnest, deadpan, committed-to-the-bit. The interface itself is the straight man: it must look like a credible professional network so the absurd content lands harder. Never wink visually; all humor lives in copy and content. Emotions: amusement, recognition ("this is exactly LinkedIn"), confidence to participate.

### Aesthetic Direction
"Serious corporate network, suspiciously polished." LinkedIn's layout grammar (top nav with icon+badge, card feed, 3-column desktop) with a distinct identity: cobalt #1f4fd8 as the brand blue (not LinkedIn's #0a66c2), warm paper #f4f1ea ground, safety-orange #e8590c accents, gold reserved for the fake Premium. Light mode only. Anti-references: generic AI dashboards, dark-mode-with-glow, purple gradients, actual LinkedIn cloning close enough to be a trademark problem.

Typography: Fraunces (display serif, old-money corporate gravitas with a hidden smirk) for the wordmark and headings; Public Sans (a literal US-government sans, the most bureaucratically earnest body font in existence) for UI and body. Both self-hosted woff2. The font pairing IS the joke told with a straight face.

### Design Principles
1. The UI plays it straight; only the words are funny.
2. Familiarity is the punchline: every pattern should be recognizable from real professional networks.
3. Density over airiness: it's a feed product; content starts above the fold.
4. One brand blue, one accent orange, gold only for Premium satire; never mix them in one component.
5. Body text 14-15px is acceptable for feed density, but never below 12px, and secondary text keeps >= 4.5:1 contrast.
